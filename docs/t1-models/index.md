!!! tip "The model is rarely the interesting part"

    Switching between reasonable algorithms moves your score a couple of points.
    The work you did in the last two groups moves it far more. Every project
    here assumes a properly prepared, properly split dataset, and will punish you
    quietly if it does not have one.
