    16 October to 14 November 4 projects

    Clean data was the training wheels. Everything here is about data that arrives in the wrong shape, from a source you do not control, with problems nobody warned you about.

    Preparation and preprocessing are not the same thing

    Preparation makes your data correct. Preprocessing makes it model-ready. You can do the first perfectly and still ruin a project with the second.
